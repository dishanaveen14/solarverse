How It Works

Three simple steps to explore the Solar System

1

Open Camera

Allow the website to access your device camera for the AR experience and hand gesture control.

2

Use Hand Gestures

Open palm to rotate, pinch to zoom, point to select a planet, and make a fist to pause.

3

Tap & Explore

Tap any planet to see its information, or click "Explore" on a planet card for full details.
